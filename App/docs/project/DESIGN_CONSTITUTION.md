# Design Constitution

Status: FIXED

This constitution controls all future visible product work. Functional needs may alter exact pixels, but not the approved visual language.

## Visual identity

- Light warm neutral background
- Charcoal or near-black typography
- Restrained muted deep teal accent
- Editorial serif used selectively for major headings and brand character
- Clean UI sans-serif for controls and body copy where appropriate
- Thin dividers, disciplined spacing, subtle borders, and modest shadows only when useful
- Rectangular controls with modest corner radius
- Notion-quality information density
- Consistent real iconography, preferably Lucide where suitable
- Practical desktop-application feel

## Logo direction

- Open-book form
- Reading side plus screen/play side
- Charcoal and muted deep teal
- Refined `Read & Watch` wordmark
- Editorial, not generic SaaS

## Permanent anti-vibe rules

Never use:

- Purple gradients, purple startup language, or gratuitous gradients
- Pill-shaped primary buttons or pill-shaped navigation everywhere
- Fake reviews, testimonials, social proof, customer logos, usage counts, growth numbers, metrics, account avatars, or user accounts
- Meaningless marketing hero text or generic AI-generated startup copy
- Emoji as application icons
- Em dash characters in user-facing UI copy
- AI stock photography, AI-generated fake humans, or generic stock-photo hero sections
- Cursor-follow or fake typewriter/cursor animations
- Gratuitous parallax, excessive motion, or over-the-top scroll animation
- Decorative glassmorphism, neon glow cards, or giant decorative blobs
- Trend components without functional value
- Invented content presented as real user data

Use restrained motion only when it improves orientation or interaction feedback.

## Product truthfulness

Privacy, Terms, settings, empty states, and product copy must describe implemented reality. Do not imply accounts, cloud processing, sharing, collaboration, sync, or metrics that do not exist.

## Verification

Every UI phase requires desktop and constrained-width checks, keyboard behavior, accessibility review proportional to scope, empty/error/loading states, console inspection, and a constitution review recorded in its phase report.
